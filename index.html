Model and equations

Pagani & Carrera (2016)›

The fuselage is split into N rigid compartments, each with uniform pressure, density and temperature (zero-dimensional model). Air flows through breaches and vents, treated as quasi-1D orifices with an effective area Aeff = CDA. The ambient is an infinite reservoir. The breach appears instantaneously at t = 0.

Mass balance

dρidt=1Vi(m˙in−m˙out)(7)

Orifice flow

Flow from i to j is choked (sonic throat) when pi ≥ pi* = ((γ+1)/2)γ/(γ−1) pj = 1.893 pj:

m˙ij*=ρi(2γ+1)1γ−1Aeff2γRTiγ+1(4)

otherwise the flow is subsonic:

m˙ij=Aeff2piρiγγ−1[(pjpi)2γ−(pjpi)γ+1γ](5)

Compartment state

TiTc0=(pipc0)n−1n=(ρiρc0)n−1(8)

n = γ = 1.4 gives the isentropic model of the paper. n = 1.16 is the polytropic exponent measured by Haber and Clamann (1953); n = 1 is isothermal (Mavriplis 1963).

Hinged blowout panel

d2θdt2=3ΔpApcos2θ2mpb,Aopen=Ap(1−cosθ)(12–13)

Translational blowout panel

d2xdt2=ΔpApmp,Aopen=min[2(Apb+b)x,Ap](15–16)

A panel stays shut until the differential across it exceeds its release pressure prel. After release it moves under the instantaneous differential. All equations are integrated with the explicit Euler scheme of Eqs. (17)–(18).

Reported quantities

  • Supercritical phase: time until every compartment falls below p* = 1.893 pa, as in Tables 1 and 2 of the paper. The time during which the breach itself is choked is also listed.
  • Total decompression time: time when every compartment is within the equalisation tolerance of ambient pressure.
  • Panel opening time: from release until the open area reaches its maximum.
  • Partition load: peak |Δp| multiplied by the partition area given on a vent.
  • Cabin pressure altitude: ISA altitude with the same static pressure. CS-25 limits cabin altitude to 2438 m (8000 ft) in normal operation.
